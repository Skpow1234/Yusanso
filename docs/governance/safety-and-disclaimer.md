# Safety and disclaimer

> **This platform produces research and engineering artifacts. It is not a medical device and must not be marketed or used as a certified clinical diagnosis service.**

## Rules

1. Inference responses include technical metadata only (model version, task, scores, request ID)
2. Do not log or expose PHI in metrics, traces, dashboards, or model cards
3. Restricted datasets (e.g. full MIMIC) never enter public CI — use synthetic fixtures
4. Promotion gates use engineering/research regression thresholds, not invented clinical cutoffs
5. Align future clinical claims work with applicable guidance (WHO AI ethics, FDA CDS, local regulation)
